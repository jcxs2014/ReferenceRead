# 98. Vocabulary — 学术词汇表

## A. Logical connectors + 原文例句

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| in both ... and ... | 'plays a crucial role in both the processes of acceleration and transport' | 并列，指出同一机制的两个作用 |
| mainly in the form of | 'such action is mainly in two forms' | 引出枚举框架 |
| in the direction of | 'mainly in the direction of exploring non-linear aspects' | 指出研究趋势方向 |
| namely | 'namely the action of cosmic rays on the environment' | 精确化前词 |
| such as | 'such as the B/C ratio and the p=p ratio' | 举例具体化 |
| leading to | 'through streaming instabilities, leading to a dependence of the scattering properties' | 因果链递进 |

## B. 领域术语

- **Streaming instability**：CR streaming 激发的 Alfvén 波不稳定性，是自洽散射的物理基础
- **B/C ratio**：硼/碳比——二级粒子（硼由碳轰击产生）是扩散系数的探针
- **Diffusion coefficient $D(E) \propto E^{\delta}$**：能量依赖扩散，$\delta \approx 0.3-0.6$
- **NLDSA（Non-Linear Diffusive Shock Acceleration）**：CR 反馈改变激波结构的非线性加速理论
- **Galactic winds**：CR 对等离子体的动量反馈驱动的外流
- **p/p ratio**：质子/反质子比——暗物质信号载体
- **e+/e- ratio**：正电子/电子比——暗物质或脉冲星贡献的探针
- **Continuous escape**：CR 通过连续泄露而非瞬时逃逸的机制

## C. 长难句分析

**Abstract 第2句**：
> 'Recent theoretical developments in the field of cosmic ray research have been mainly in the direction of exploring non-linear aspects of the processes in which these particles are involved, namely the action of cosmic rays on the environment in which the transport and/or acceleration take place.'

- 句型：主语 + 介词短语前置 + 名词性从句
- 逻辑：研究趋势 + 精确化（namely）+ 括号补充说明
- 结构：non-linear aspects = action of CRs on the environment
