## C. 长难句摘录

### C1. §1（正电子比率异常的核心句）

> "Even more surprising are the recent findings concerning the anti-matter component of CRs: an unexpected rise with energy in the positron to electron ratio has been detected by both the PAMELA and AMS-02 experiment."

**主干**：an unexpected rise + has been detected + by both PAMELA and AMS-02
**修饰**：Even more surprising（强调），the anti-matter component of CRs（同位语），rise with energy（定语）
**翻译**：更令人惊讶的是最近关于宇宙线反物质成分的研究：正电子/电子比率随能量的意外上升已被 PAMELA 和 AMS-02 两个实验都探测到。
**逻辑功能**：强调观测的意外性——"even more surprising"是前文质子谱异常的递进。

### C2. §3（CR 不是被动者的核心句）

> "The emerging picture is one in which CRs are far from passive spectators of their acceleration and transport through the Galaxy. They rather affect the environment that hosts them in two main ways."

**主干**：The emerging picture + is one + in which + CRs + are + far from passive spectators
**修饰**：far from passive spectators（否定短语作表语），rather affect（转折强调）
**翻译**：正在形成的图景是：CR 远非其加速和传播过程中的被动旁观者，而是以两种主要方式影响承载它们的环境。
**逻辑功能**：本文核心论点——CR 是主动者而非被动者。

### C3. §4（$D(p)$ 断裂的物理起源句）

> "It is a non-trivial conclusion, given that in this model, the breaks in the spectra of all nuclei at rigidity $\sim 100-1000$ GV arise from the transition from a Kolmogorov-like diffusion coefficient to an energy independent one, rather than from the injection spectrum."

**主干**：the breaks + arise from + the transition + rather than from + the injection spectrum
**修饰**：non-trivial conclusion（评价），given that...（条件状语从句），at rigidity $\sim 100-1000$ GV（状语）
**翻译**：这是一个非平凡的结论——因为在此模型中，所有核素在 $\sim 100-1000$ GV 处的能谱断裂源于扩散系数从 Kolmogorov 型向能量无关型的过渡，而非来自注入谱。
**逻辑功能**：区分"唯象"和"物理"断裂——本文最重要论点之一。
