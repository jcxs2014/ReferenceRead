# 98. Vocabulary — Mewaldt et al. (2001)

## A. Logical connectors

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| There are a number of | 'There are a number of radioactive "clocks" in the cosmic radiation' | 引出工具 |
| With high-resolution isotope measurements available from ACE | 'With high-resolution isotope measurements available from ACE it is now possible' | 条件成立 |
| It is also generally accepted that | 'It is also generally accepted that the mechanism... is shock acceleration' | 共识确认 |

## B. 领域术语

- **Be-10（半衰期 1.5 Myr）**：宇宙线中的放射性同位素，测量停留时间
- **Al-26（半衰期 0.7 Myr）**：探测 freshly accelerated material 的同位素时钟
- **Cl-36（半衰期 0.3 Myr）**：短时间尺度过程探针
- **ACE（Advanced Composition Explorer）**：NASA 探测器，携带高分辨率质谱仪
- **B/C 比值**：硼/碳比，CR 传播的经典探针（但比值无时间量纲）
- **Reacceleration**：再加速，扩散过程中 CR 获得能量（与 DLA 不同）
- **Convection**：对流，CR 随星际风向外迁移
