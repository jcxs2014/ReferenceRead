## C. 长难句拆解

**例 1**（§1）：
> "What is the origin of the material that gets accelerated? Do cosmic rays originate from fresh supernova ejecta, 'old' interstellar material, or a combination of these?"

结构：三个并列疑问句。"the material that gets accelerated" 是关系从句修饰 "origin"。"fresh ejecta / old ISM / combination" 是三种假设来源的枚举。

**例 2**（§4）：
> "In such models the distance (R) from which radioactive species can arrive is limited by its half-life, such that $R \approx (D \Gamma T_d)^{1/2}$, where D is the diffusion coefficient, $T_d$ is the mean life for radioactive decay at rest, and $\Gamma = 1/(1-\beta^2)^{1/2}$."

结构："In such models" 状语 → 主语 "the distance (R) from which..."（含关系从句）→ 被动 "is limited by its half-life" → "such that..." 引导结果从句给出公式 → "where..." 定义三个变量。
