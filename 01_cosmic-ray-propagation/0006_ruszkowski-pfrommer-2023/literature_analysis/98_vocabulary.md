## C. 长难句摘录

### C1.

> C1. 'The appealing features of cosmic rays are their relatively long cooling times, on the order of 10^9 yr for GeV protons, which is comparable to or longer than the dynamical times of galaxies and galaxy clusters, allowing CRs to accumulate and exert sustained pressure on the ambient plasma.' (~60 words; which 引导非限制定语 + allowing 现在分词)


### C2.

> C2. 'The CR-driven galactic wind model posits that cosmic rays, accelerated by supernovae in the disk, stream along magnetic field lines and exert pressure on the ambient gas, driving a multiphase outflow that carries metals into the circumgalactic medium.' (~45 words; 三层并列 that 从句)


### C3.

> C3. 'Although the detailed microphysics of CR injection from supernova remnants into the interstellar medium remains uncertain, the global picture of CR feedback has converged on a coherent framework that explains both the observed galactic winds and the suppression of cooling flows in galaxy clusters.' (~50 words; 让步 + 平行并列)
