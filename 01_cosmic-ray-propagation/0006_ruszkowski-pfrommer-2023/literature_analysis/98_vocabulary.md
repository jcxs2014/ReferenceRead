# 98. Vocabulary — Ruszkowski & Pfrommer (2023)

## A. Logical connectors

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| The appealing features of | 'The appealing features of CRs are their relatively long cooling times' | 引出优势 |
| Similarly | 'Similarly, CRs may provide a significant contribution to' | 类比延伸 |
| Overall | 'Overall, the underlying physics of CR interactions with plasmas' | 归纳总结 |

## B. 领域术语

- **Feedback**：反馈，CR 对星系/星系团演化的动力学影响
- **Cooling flow problem**：冷却流问题——星系团核心为何没大量冷却形成恒星
- **Galactic wind**：银河风，CR 压力驱动的外流
- **CGM/ICM**： Circumgalactic/Intracluster Medium，星系周围/星系团内热气体
- **Streaming instability**：流动不稳定性，CR 流出时激发磁场不稳定性
- **Equipartition**：能量均分，CR 与热/磁场能量密度相当
- **Fermi bubbles**：银河系中心 Fermi-LAT 观测到的 γ 射线气泡结构
