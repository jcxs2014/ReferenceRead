## C. 长难句摘录（3–5 句）

### C1. [§1 Introduction]

> The standard paradigm of the origin of cosmic rays (CRs) is based on three main pillars.

**主干**：The standard paradigm is based on three main pillars.
**修饰**：of the origin of cosmic rays (CRs) 修饰 paradigm
**翻译**：宇宙线（CR）起源的标准范式基于三个主要支柱。

---

### C2. [§1 Introduction]

> The CR residence time in such a halo decreases with rigidity as $\tau_{\rm res}(R) = H^2/D(R) \propto R^{-\delta}$ ($\delta \approx 0.3$-0.6).

**主干**：The CR residence time decreases with rigidity.
**修饰**：in such a halo 修饰 residence time；as $\tau_{\rm res}(R) = H^2/D(R) \propto R^{-\delta}$ 修饰 decreases；($\delta \approx 0.3$-0.6) 修饰 $\delta$
**翻译**：CR 在这种晕中的停留时间随刚性减小，$\tau_{\rm res}(R) = H^2/D(R) \propto R^{-\delta}$（$\delta \approx 0.3$-0.6）。

### C3. [§2 Model]

> The particle transport perpendicular to B0 is a combination of the FLRW, namely the stochastic motion of field lines, which is due to fluctuations on a large-scale (much larger than the resonant scale) and of the diffusion across field lines due to particle jumping from one line to another.

**主干**：The particle transport is a combination of the FLRW and of the diffusion.
**修饰**：perpendicular to B0 修饰 transport；namely the stochastic motion of field lines 修饰 FLRW；which is due to fluctuations on a large-scale 修饰 motion；(much larger than the resonant scale) 修饰 large-scale；due to particle jumping from one line to another 修饰 diffusion
**翻译**：垂直于 B0 的粒子输运是 FLRW（即磁场线的随机运动，由大尺度（远大于共振尺度）的涨落引起）和由于粒子从一条线跳到另一条线而跨越磁场线的扩散的组合。

### C5. [§3 Results]

> The combination of a hard $Q_{\bar{p}}$ and of the progressive flattening of the grammage $X$ keeps $|\gamma_{\bar{p}} - \gamma_p| \lesssim 0.15$ in the range $R = 30$-1000 GV, which translates in a very mild decrease in the $\bar{p}/p$ ratio in such rigidity range.

**主干**：The combination keeps $|\gamma_{\bar{p}} - \gamma_p| \lesssim 0.15$.
**修饰**：of a hard $Q_{\bar{p}}$ and of the progressive flattening of the grammage $X$ 修饰 combination；in the range $R = 30$-1000 GV 修饰 keeps；which translates in a very mild decrease in the $\bar{p}/p$ ratio 修饰整个句子；in such rigidity range 修饰 decrease
**翻译**：硬的 $Q_{\bar{p}}$ 和 grammage $X$ 的渐进变浅的组合使得 $|\gamma_{\bar{p}} - \gamma_p| \lesssim 0.15$ 在 $R = 30$-1000 GV 范围内，这转化为 $\bar{p}/p$ 比在该刚性范围内的非常温和的下降。
