## C. 长难句摘录

### C1.

> C1. 'Approximately half of the elements beyond the iron peak are produced by the slow neutron capture process (s process) in helium-burning regions of evolved stars, while the other half, together with the actinides, are synthesized by the rapid neutron capture process (r process), which requires extreme neutron fluxes obtainable only in explosive astrophysical events.' (~60 words; while + which 复合从句)


### C2.

> C2. 'The discovery of the binary neutron star merger GW170817 in August 2017 and the subsequent identification of its kilonova counterpart AT2017gfo provided the first direct observational evidence that neutron star mergers are prolific sites of r-process nucleosynthesis, synthesizing both the lighter r-process elements through the first peak and the heaviest actinides.' (~55 words; 长主语 + 复合谓语)


### C3.

> C3. 'Despite the tremendous progress achieved in our understanding of the r process since Burbidge, Burbidge, Fowler, and Hoyle proposed the original framework in 1957, several open questions remain, particularly regarding the relative contributions of different astrophysical sites, the detailed distribution of yields as a function of electron fraction, and the nuclear physics inputs that determine the r-process path through the chart of nuclides.' (~75 words; 三层并列分词状语)
