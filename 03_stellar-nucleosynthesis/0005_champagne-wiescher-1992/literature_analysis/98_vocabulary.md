## C. 长难句摘录（3–5 句）

### C1. §3.6（激发态贡献）
> "The rate of reactions proceeding through a resonance may be significantly altered in a stellar environment relative to that measured in the laboratory because of **Boltzmann population of excited states** and **stellar beta-decay rates**."

**主干**：rate may be altered in a stellar environment relative to that measured in the laboratory
**修饰**：because of … 引导原因状语（Boltzmann 激发态布居 + 恒星 $\beta$ 衰变率）；proceeding through a resonance 作后置定语修饰 reactions
**翻译**：在恒星环境下，经由共振进行的反应速率可能与实验室中测得的速率显著不同，原因在于激发态的玻尔兹曼布居和恒星 $\beta$ 衰变率与实验室条件不同。

### C2. §4.5.1（等待点端点）
> "If the rp-process proceeds for less than 17 s, it will terminate at **$^{72}{\rm Kr}$ ($T_{1}$/₂ = 17.2 s)**, whose half-life imposes a **time gate** on whether the process can reach the A ≈ 70 region."

**主干**：it will terminate at $^{72}{\rm Kr}$
**修饰**：If … 条件状语从句；whose half-life imposes a time gate on whether … 非限定定语从句进一步刻画 $^{72}{\rm Kr}$ 的物理意义
**翻译**：若 rp-过程持续时间不足 17 s，它将终止于 $^{72}{\rm Kr}$（$T_{1}$/₂ = 17.2 s），该核素的半衰期构成一个"时间门"，决定了过程能否到达 A≈70 区域。

### C3. §5.3（端点）
> "At **$T_{9}$ = 1.5**, the reaction flow proceeds continuously from **$^{4}{\rm He}$ to $^{73}{\rm Kr}$** via a combination of **proton captures** and **beta decays** near the proton drip line, **until** the path is halted by the particle-unbound nature of **$^{73}{\rm Rb}$**."

**主干**：reaction flow proceeds continuously from $^{4}{\rm He}$ to $^{73}{\rm Kr}$
**修饰**：At $T_{9}$=1.5 条件状语；via a combination of … 方式状语；until … 时间状语从句给出终止条件
**翻译**：在 $T_{9}$ = 1.5 下，反应流从 $^{4}{\rm He}$ 连续推进至 $^{73}{\rm Kr}$，途经质子滴线附近的质子俘获与 $\beta$ 衰变组合，直至 $^{73}{\rm Rb}$ 的粒子未束缚性质阻断路径。

### C4. §6 结语（沙上建塔）
> "Although an impressive amount of effort has been devoted to understanding nucleosynthesis during explosive hydrogen burning, **a great deal of work remains to be done**; **based on our knowledge of reaction rates, it is fair to ask if we have not built a house of cards.**"

**主干**：a great deal of work remains to be done；it is fair to ask if …
**修饰**：Although … 让步状语从句；based on … 方式状语；if 引导同位语从句作为 ask 的宾语
**翻译**：尽管已有大量工作致力于理解爆炸性氢燃烧中的核合成，但仍有许多工作有待完成；基于我们对手中反应率的了解，问一问"我们是不是在用沙子上建塔"是合乎情理的。

### C5. §2.2（反应率现状核心判断）
> "Because of these large uncertainties, **models of cataclysmic binaries rely on rather suspect nuclear reaction input**."

**主干**：models of cataclysmic binaries rely on nuclear reaction input
**修饰**：Because of these large uncertainties 原因状语前置；rather suspect 作前置形容词修饰 input
**翻译**：由于这些巨大的不确定度，灾变双星模型所依赖的核反应输入数据是相当可疑的。
