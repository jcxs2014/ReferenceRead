## C. 长难句摘录（3–5 句）

### C1. §2.8（原始太阳重建）
> "Using the Boothroyd & Sackmann (2003) scaling relations between **Z/X** and **Y, X, Z₀, X₀, Y₀**, and adopting the new photospheric metallicity **Z/X = 0.0177**, one obtains **X₀ = 0.7110 ± 0.0040**, **Y₀ = 0.2741 ± 0.0120**, **Z₀ = 0.0149 ± 0.0015**; the conversion between present-day and protosolar abundances is **A(El)₀ = A(El) + 0.074 dex** for all elements above He, corresponding to **~16% settling of heavy elements** and **~18% settling of helium**."

**主干**：one obtains X₀, Y₀, Z₀; the conversion is A(El)₀ = A(El) + 0.074
**修饰**：Using … 方式状语；and adopting … 并列方式状语；corresponding to … 现在分词结果状语
**翻译**：利用 Boothroyd & Sackmann (2003) 关于 Z/X 与 Y、X、Z₀、X₀、Y₀ 的标度关系，并采用新的光球金属度 Z/X = 0.0177，可得 X₀ = 0.7110 ± 0.0040、Y₀ = 0.2741 ± 0.0120、Z₀ = 0.0149 ± 0.0015；对 He 以上所有元素，现今与原始丰度之间的转换关系为 A(El)₀ = A(El) + 0.074 dex，对应约 16% 的重元素沉降与约 18% 的氦沉降。

### C2. §3.1（fO₂ 下降）
> "Because **H₂O/H₂ ≈ 5.0 × 10⁻⁴**, about half the Anders & Grevesse (1989) value of 9.2 × 10⁻⁴, the **new fO₂ is lower**, and **all oxide and silicate condensation temperatures are consequently reduced** by roughly 10 K — a direct consequence of the depressed C, N, O abundances."

**主干**：new fO₂ is lower; condensation temperatures are consequently reduced by ~10 K
**修饰**：Because … 原因状语；— 后的破折号补充说明因果关系
**翻译**：由于 H₂O/H₂ ≈ 5.0 × 10⁻⁴，约为 Anders & Grevesse (1989) 值 9.2 × 10⁻⁴ 的一半，新的 fO₂ 更低，所有氧化物与硅酸盐冷凝温度因此降低约 10 K——这是 C、N、O 丰度下调的直接结果。

### C3. §1.3（触发本文的直接原因）
> "**Allende Prieto, Lambert & Asplund (2001, 2002)** have **substantially revised downward** the solar **C and O** abundances (by a factor 1.4–1.7 relative to Anders & Grevesse 1989); since **C and O control the chemistry of most other elements**, the **absolute O abundance** lowers the condensation temperatures of O-bearing compounds, and the **C/O ratio** (now ~0.50, vs. 0.49 for Grevesse & Sauval 1998 and 0.42 for Anders & Grevesse 1989) further shifts the condensate distribution from oxides/silicates toward C-bearing compounds."

**主干**：Allende Prieto et al. have revised downward the solar C and O abundances; C and O control chemistry; absolute O lowers condensation T; C/O ratio shifts condensate distribution
**修饰**：since … 原因状语从句；two parallel effects 分别由 absolute O 和 C/O ratio 引出
**翻译**：Allende Prieto, Lambert & Asplund (2001, 2002) 已大幅下调太阳 C 和 O 的丰度（相对 Anders & Grevesse 1989 约 1.4–1.7 倍）；由于 C 和 O 控制大多数其他元素的化学，绝对 O 丰度降低会使含氧化合物冷凝温度下降，而 C/O 比（现约 0.50，对比 Grevesse & Sauval 1998 的 0.49 与 Anders & Grevesse 1989 的 0.42）进一步使冷凝物分布从氧化物/硅酸盐向含碳化合物偏移。

### C4. §3.1（CONDOR 方法）
> "**CONDOR** treats **2000 gas-phase species** and **1600 condensates** of **all 83 natural elements** simultaneously, **converging** each element's computed abundance to its input value to **1 part in 10¹⁵**; **this is essential** because trace-element chemistry is strongly influenced by the chemistry of the major-element host phases."

**主干**：CONDOR treats 2000 gas-phase species and 1600 condensates simultaneously
**修饰**：converging … 现在分词方式状语；this is essential 主句补充；because 原因状语从句
**翻译**：CONDOR 同时处理 2000 种气相物种和 1600 种凝聚相的 83 种天然元素，将每个元素计算出的丰度与输入值收敛到 10⁻¹⁵ 的精度；这一点至关重要，因为微量元素的化学强烈受主量元素宿主相化学的影响。

### C5. §3.2（主量元素冷凝顺序）
> "The condensation sequence in the solar nebula begins with **Ca-Al-Ti ultrarefractory phases** at **~1677 K** (corundum, hibonite) — the likely precursors of **CAIs** — **followed by** forsterite and enstatite (Mg, Si) at **~1350 K**, **Fe alloy** at **~1357 K**, **troilite** (S) at **704 K**, **magnetite** at **371 K**, and **water ice** at **182 K**; the entire sequence is shifted by **~10 K** when using atmospheric rather than protosolar abundances."

**主干**：condensation sequence begins with Ca-Al-Ti phases, followed by forsterite, Fe alloy, troilite, magnetite, water ice; sequence is shifted by ~10 K
**修饰**：破折号给出 CAIs 关联；and 引导一长串 by X at Y 的并列状语；when using … 条件状语从句
**翻译**：太阳星云中的冷凝顺序始于约 1677 K 的 Ca-Al-Ti 超难熔相（刚玉、金红石）——很可能是 CAIs 的前驱——随后是约 1350 K 的镁橄榄石和镁灰石（Mg、Si）、约 1357 K 的 Fe 合金、704 K 的陨硫石（S）、371 K 的磁铁矿，以及 182 K 的水冰；当使用大气而非原始太阳丰度时，整个序列整体下移约 10 K。
