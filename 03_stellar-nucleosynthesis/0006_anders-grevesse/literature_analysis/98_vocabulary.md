## C. 长难句摘录（3–5 句）

### C1. §7.2（陨石-太阳一致性）
> "The **overall** agreement between photospheric and meteoritic abundances is **extremely good**; **29 elements with sufficient spectroscopic quality** agree to **±9%** (±0.036 dex), **although** five so-called 'problem elements' (Ge, Pb, W, Fe, Mn) deviate beyond what their solar data quality alone can account for."

**主干**：agreement is extremely good; 29 elements agree to ±9%
**修饰**：although … 让步从句引入"问题元素"；although 后接长定语 beyond what their solar data quality alone can account for
**翻译**：光球与陨石丰度总体一致性极好；29 个有足够光谱质量的元素一致到 ±9%（±0.036 dex），尽管有 5 个所谓"问题元素"（Ge、Pb、W、Fe、Mn）的偏离已超出其太阳数据质量单独所能解释的范围。

### C2. §7.1（FIP 分馏核心）
> "Coronal abundances show a clear elemental fractionation, **with the first ionization potential (FIP) as the key parameter**: abundances drop by a factor of **≈ 4.5 (−0.65 dex)** at **FIP ≈ 9.5–11 eV**, producing a low-FIP plateau (K–Zn) and a high-FIP plateau (C–Ne) that is systematically depressed."

**主干**：abundances drop by a factor of ≈ 4.5 at FIP ≈ 9.5–11 eV
**修饰**：with … as the key parameter 伴随状语；producing … 现在分词结果状语；two appositives low-FIP plateau 与 high-FIP plateau
**翻译**：日冕丰度呈现清晰的元素分馏，以第一电离势（FIP）为关键参数：在 FIP ≈ 9.5–11 eV 处丰度骤降约 4.5 倍（−0.65 dex），形成低 FIP 平台（K–Zn）与系统性压低的高 FIP 平台（C–Ne）。

### C3. §7.4（平滑性失效）
> "The **smoothness hypothesis** which has guided this field for 40 years **has now reached its limits of validity** and **should be retired with honor**: **at the 10–20% level**, odd-A isotope-abundance curves are irregular **because** most nuclides in this mass range are **synthesized by a mixture of s and r processes** whose individual contributions are themselves not smooth functions of mass number."

**主干**：hypothesis has reached its limits and should be retired
**修饰**：which … 定语从句说明 40 年指导作用；because … 原因状语从句；whose 进一步定语从句
**翻译**：指导本领域 40 年的平滑性假设现已到达其适用极限，应"荣退"：在 10–20% 的水平上奇质量数核素丰度曲线不规则，是因为该质量区大多数核素由 s 过程和 r 过程混合合成，而这两种过程的贡献本身也不是质量数的平滑函数。

### C4. §7.2（$Fe_{3}$$O_{4}$ 磁铁矿机制）
> "A mechanism **unique to Fe** is proposed: at temperatures **below 400 K**, the stable chemical form of Fe is **magnetite $Fe_{3}$$O_{4}$**, which may act as the **principal condensation phase of Fe** throughout the entire gravitational collapse of the interstellar cloud, **and can be fractionated from other solids by ferromagnetism**."

**主干**：A mechanism unique to Fe is proposed
**修饰**：which may act as … 定语从句；and can be fractionated 并列谓语；at temperatures below 400 K 条件状语
**翻译**：提出了一种铁独有的机制：在温度低于 400 K 时，铁的稳定化学形式是磁铁矿 $Fe_{3}$$O_{4}$，它可能在星际云整个引力坍缩过程中作为铁的主要凝聚相，并可通过铁磁性从其他固体中分馏出来。

### C5. §3.3（尺度耦合）
> "The scale coupling between the **cosmochemical scale** (Si = $10^{6}$) and the **astronomical scale** (log H = 12) is obtained by averaging log(Sun/Met) for **12 well-determined elements** (Na, Mg, Si, Ca, V, Cr, Co, Ni, Y, Zr, Nb, Mo), yielding **R = 1.554 ± 0.020** with a formal uncertainty of only ±5%."

**主干**：the scale coupling is obtained by averaging log(Sun/Met) for 12 elements, yielding R = 1.554 ± 0.020
**修饰**：两个括号分别定义两种标尺；with a formal uncertainty … 结果状语
**翻译**：宇宙化学标尺（Si = $10^{6}$）与天文标尺（log H = 12）之间的尺度耦合，通过对 12 个测定良好的元素（Na、Mg、Si、Ca、V、Cr、Co、Ni、Y、Zr、Nb、Mo）取 log(Sun/Met) 均值得到，结果为 R = 1.554 ± 0.020，形式不确定度仅 ±5%。
