## C. 长难句摘录（5 句）

### C1. [§Abstract]
> "In the former, the variations are attributed to the dredgeup of material that has been processed through the CNO cycle in the globular-cluster stars themselves. In the latter, the variations are attributed to primordial chemical inhomogeneities in the material out of which the cluster stars were formed, the composition of these 'clumps' having been determined by nuclear processing in a prior generation of more massive stars."

**主干**：In the former, the variations are attributed to X. In the latter, the variations are attributed to Y.
**修饰**：
- that has been processed through the CNO cycle... 修饰 material
- in the globular-cluster stars themselves 定位场所
- out of which the cluster stars were formed 修饰 material（原始物质）
- the composition... having been determined by... 独立主格，说明 clumps 组成的来源
**翻译**：在前者（演化场景）中，丰度差异归因于团星自身 CNO 循环处理过物质的挖升；在后者（原始场景）中，差异归因于团星形成物质的原始化学不均匀性——这些"团块"的组成由先前一代更大质量恒星的核处理决定。

### C2. [§3]
> "The preceding sketch would suggest that, until we get to stages past the He core and/or shell flashes, little change in the surface abundances is expected, and indeed only tiny modifications are predicted during the first ascent of the RGB."

**主干**：The sketch would suggest that little change is expected, and only tiny modifications are predicted.
**修饰**：
- until we get to stages past the He core and/or shell flashes 时间状语从句，限定"预言微小变化"的适用阶段
- indeed 强调
- during the first ascent of the RGB 补充位置
**翻译**：前述简述表明，直到我们越过 He 核闪和/或壳层闪阶段之前，表面丰度预期变化很小，事实上在 RGB 第一攀升阶段预言的仅是极微小的修改。

### C3. [§5]
> "The Sweigart-Mengel theory leads to several predictions that can be tested by the observations, for example, evidence of mixing can appear in the atmosphere of an immediate post-main-sequence giant only after the CNO shell crosses the radius corresponding to the deepest penetration of the external convection zone."

**主干**：The theory leads to several predictions that can be tested.
**修饰**：
- for example, evidence of mixing can appear... only after... 举例说明
- corresponding to the deepest penetration of the external convection zone 修饰 radius
**翻译**：Sweigart-Mengel 理论引出若干可被观测检验的预言，例如，在刚刚离开主序列的巨星大气中，混合证据只能在 CNO 壳层越过对应于外部对流层最深穿透的半径之后才会出现。

### C4. [§6]
> "A more serious difficulty for a scenario in which the abundance changes are driven solely by stellar evolution is the finding of a CN versus CH anticorrelation (on average) among main-sequence stars in NGC 6752 and 47 Tuc."

**主干**：A more serious difficulty for [a scenario in which X is driven solely by Y] is the finding of Z.
**修饰**：
- in which the abundance changes are driven solely by stellar evolution 修饰 scenario
- (on average) 限定反相关的统计性质
- among main-sequence stars in NGC 6752 and 47 Tuc 定位发现场所
**翻译**：对于"丰度变化仅由恒星演化驱动"这一场景而言，一个更严重的问题是：在 NGC 6752 与 47 Tuc 的主序星中，观测到 CN 与 CH 呈（平均）反相关。

### C5. [§7]
> "One can understand qualitatively the shape of the O versus Na anticorrelation, i.e., why one finds an 'arclike' distribution of points (Fig. 8): as the abundant species ($^{16}{\rm O}$ and $^{20}{\rm Ne}$) are transformed into the initially rare species ($^{14}{\rm N}$ and $^{23}{\rm Na}$), the latter at first increase very rapidly, but then the rate of increase levels off as the initially more abundant species become more and more heavily depleted."

**主干**：One can understand qualitatively the shape of the anticorrelation: as A is transformed into B, B at first increases rapidly, but then the rate levels off.
**修饰**：
- i.e., why one finds an 'arclike' distribution of points 举例说明
- ($^{16}{\rm O}$ and $^{20}{\rm Ne}$) / ($^{14}{\rm N}$ and $^{23}{\rm Na}$) 具体列出"丰富"与"稀少"物种
- as the initially more abundant species become more and more heavily depleted 时间状语从句，解释增长率趋缓的原因
**翻译**：人们可以定性理解 O-Na 反相关的形状，即为何在图 8 中看到"弧形"分布点：当丰富物种（$^{16}{\rm O}$ 与 $^{20}{\rm Ne}$）转化为最初稀少的物种（$^{14}{\rm N}$ 与 $^{23}{\rm Na}$）时，后者起初急剧增加，但随后最初更丰富的物种越来越被耗竭，增长率也随之趋缓。
