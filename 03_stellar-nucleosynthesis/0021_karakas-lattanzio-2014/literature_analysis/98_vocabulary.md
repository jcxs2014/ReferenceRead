## C. 长难句（Complex sentences）

**句 1**（§3.3.1）：
> "The general trend is that λ increases with increasing stellar mass, at a given Z; the parameter λ also increases with decreasing metallicity, at a given mass. Naively, this means that it should be easier to make C stars in lower metallicity or higher mass models."
- 主干：The general trend is [that λ increases with mass / λ increases with decreasing Z]
- 修饰："at a given Z" 限定；"Naively, this means..." 引入推论
- 中文：大趋势是 λ 随恒星质量增大而增大（给定 Z）；λ 也随金属度降低而增大（给定质量）。直觉上这意味着在低金属度或高质量模型中更容易形成 C 星。

**句 2**（§3.5）：
> "During a thermal pulse some of the ⁴He in the shell is converted into ¹²C by partial He burning, leaving the composition of the well-mixed intershell approximately 70–75% ⁴He (by mass), 20–25% ¹²C and a few percent ¹⁶O."
- 主干：⁴He is converted into ¹²C
- 修饰：During a thermal pulse（时状）；by partial He burning（方式状）；leaving…（现在分词结果状语）
- 中文：在一次热脉冲期间，壳中部分 ⁴He 经部分 He 燃烧转为 ¹²C，使完全混合的 intershell 组成大致为 ⁴He 70–75%（质量）、¹²C 20–25%、¹⁶O 几%。

**句 3**（§3.7）：
> "The s-process will therefore produce isotopes along the valley of β-stability and is responsible for the production of roughly half of all elements heavier than Fe."
- 主干：The s-process will produce isotopes / [it] is responsible for the production
- 修饰：therefore（因果）；along the valley of β-stability（状语）
- 中文：因此 s-过程将沿 β 稳定谷产生同位素，并负责约一半 Fe 以上重元素的生成。
