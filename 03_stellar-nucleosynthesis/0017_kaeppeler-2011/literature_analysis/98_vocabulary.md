## A. 学术逻辑词（标注逻辑功能）

|-----------|---------|---------|

| **The key to** | 'The key to this problem lies in' | 引出关键 |
| **In addition to** | 'In addition to the main component' | 补充说明 |

## C. 长难句摘录

### C1.

> C1. 'In the framework of the classical s-process approach, the neutron capture cross sections of all nuclei along the s-path are required for a quantitative analysis, and the respective Maxwellian-averaged cross sections have to be determined with uncertainties well below 10%.' (~50 words; 复合定语)


### C2.

> C2. 'The s process is unique among stellar nucleosynthesis mechanisms in that the time scale for neutron capture is long compared to most β-decay half-lives, so that the reaction path follows the valley of stability except at the branching points where the half-life is comparable to the mean neutron capture time.' (~55 words; 让步从句 + where 引导)


### C3.

> C3. 'Although the overall abundance pattern is a superposition of the three components—weak, main, and strong s process—the relative contributions of these components to the solar system distribution are still a matter of debate, particularly with respect to the strong component in low-metallicity stars.' (~50 words; 让步 + 三个并列)
