## C. 长难句

**1. （§2）**
> "The so-called r-process path (the magenta line in Figure 1) is then defined by the maximum abundance in all isotopic chains. After capturing a large number of neutrons these synthesized (unstable) nuclei in the path will eventually decay back to stability and form stable r-process nuclei (magenta boxes in Figure 1)."

- 主干：The r-process path is defined by the maximum abundance in all isotopic chains.
- 修饰：the magenta line in Figure 1（插入语）；After capturing…（时间状语从句）；these synthesized (unstable) nuclei in the path 是主语；will eventually decay back to stability and form stable r-process nuclei 是并列谓语。
- 译文：所谓 r-process 路径（图 1 中的洋红色线）定义为所有同位素链中的最大丰度位置。路径上的这些合成（不稳定）核在捕获大量中子后，最终 β 衰变回到稳定线，形成稳定的 r-process 核（图 1 中的洋红色方框）。

**2.（§6.2）**
> "Based on meteoritic data, Wasserburg, Busso & Gallino (1996) first suggested that the abundances of the lighter radioactive isotopes (in particular ¹²⁹I) and heavier isotopes (¹⁸²Hf) demanded different timescales for production and thus suggested two distinct r-process sites."

- 主干：Wasserburg et al. (1996) first suggested that … demanded different timescales and thus suggested two distinct sites.
- 修饰：Based on meteoritic data（方式状语）；in particular ¹²⁹I、¹⁸²Hf（插入语举例）；demanded different timescales for production（宾语从句谓语）；thus suggested two distinct sites（并列谓语）。
- 译文：基于陨石数据，Wasserburg, Busso & Gallino (1996) 首次提出较轻放射性同位素（特别是 ¹²⁹I）与较重同位素（¹⁸²Hf）的丰度要求不同的产生时标，从而暗示两个不同的 r-process 位点。

**3.（§7.2）**
> "N(T) = N₀ e^(−λT)，观测 N(T)/稳定子核 = r-only 初值 × e^(−λT)."

- 主干：指数衰变律。
- 修饰：λ = ln2/τ₁/₂（衰变常数）；T 为恒星年龄；N₀ 为 r-process 初始产额。
- 译文：放射性核丰度随时间按指数衰减；观测到的放射性核与稳定子核之比等于 r-process 初始产额乘以衰减因子。

**4.（§8.2）**
> "For this star, [La/Eu] = 0.25, indicative of a CEMP-s star with a huge r-process overabundance in addition to the s-process enhancements. To repair this deficiency, an r-process excess is assumed in the original CS 22898-027 material ([r/Fe]_ini = +2) prior to acquisition of s-process material from the AGB companion."

- 主干：[La/Eu]=0.25 → indicative of a CEMP-s star with r-process overabundance. An r-process excess is assumed prior to s-process acquisition.
- 修饰：indicative of …（过去分词作表语）；in addition to …（让步）；To repair this deficiency（目的状语）；[r/Fe]_ini = +2（括号补充）；prior to …（时间状语）。
- 译文：对该星，[La/Eu]=0.25 表明这是一颗 CEMP-s 星，除 s-process 增强外还存在巨大的 r-process 超丰。为修正这一缺陷，假设 CS 22898-027 原始物质中（从 AGB 伴星获取 s-process 材料之前）就存在 r-process 富集底（[r/Fe]_ini = +2）。

**5.（§9 结论 5）**
> "At the very lowest metallicities, the ratios for many stars are consistent with the r-process-only value, but indications of some s-processing occur already at only slightly higher metallicities."

- 主干：the ratios are consistent with the r-only value, but indications of s-processing occur at slightly higher metallicities.
- 修饰：At the very lowest metallicities（地点/金属度状语）；for many stars（范围）；already（强调时间之早）。
- 译文：在极低金属度处，许多星的 [La/Eu] 比值与纯 r-process 值一致，但仅需金属度略高一些，就已出现 s-process 介入的迹象。
