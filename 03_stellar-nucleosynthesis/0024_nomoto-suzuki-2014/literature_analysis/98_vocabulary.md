## C. 长难句 (Complex Sentences)

### C1. 电子俘获触发坍缩

**原文**："At such a high central density, the electron Fermi energy exceeds the threshold for electron captures ²⁴Mg(e⁻,ν)²⁴Na(e⁻,ν)²⁰Ne and ²⁰Ne(e⁻,ν)²⁰F(e⁻,ν)²⁰O. The resultant decrease in Ye triggers collapse (Nomoto 1987)."

**主干**：Fermi energy exceeds threshold → Ye decreases → collapse triggered
**修饰**：
- "At such a high central density" 是条件状语
- 级联反应 ²⁴Mg→²⁴Na→²⁰Ne→²⁰F→²⁰O 是机制细节
- "The resultant decrease in Ye" 中的 "resultant" 指因果链结果

**中文**：在如此高的中心密度下，电子费米能超过电子俘获阈值，发生 ²⁴Mg→²⁴Na→²⁰Ne 和 ²⁰Ne→²⁰F→²⁰O 的级联电子俘获。由此引起的 Ye 下降触发坍缩。

---

### C2. 超新星的 α-富余特征

**原文**："In complete Si-burning of hypernovae, elements produced by α-rich freeze-out are enhanced. Hence, elements synthesized through capturing α-particles, such as ⁴⁴Ti, ⁴⁸Cr, and ⁶⁴Ge (decaying into ⁴⁴Ca, ⁴⁸Ti, and ⁶⁴Zn, respectively) are more abundant."

**主干**：α-rich freeze-out → enhanced α-capture products
**修饰**：
- "In complete Si-burning of hypernovae" 限定机制与爆炸类型
- 括号内说明 α-俘获产物的放射性衰变链

**中文**：在超新星的完全 Si 燃烧中，由 α-富余冻结产生的元素被增强。因此，通过 α-粒子俘获合成的元素（如 ⁴⁴Ti、⁴⁸Cr、⁶⁴Ge，分别衰变为 ⁴⁴Ca、⁴⁸Ti、⁶⁴Zn）更丰。

---

### C3. 空洞机制解释 CEMP

**原文**："The faint SNe occur as a result of fallback of a large amount of radioactive ⁵⁶Ni whose decay into Fe powers the SN light curve (Fig. 1 right). Since the fallback of carbon is less than Fe because of the outer location of C, large [C/Fe] results."

**主干**：⁵⁶Ni fallback → Fe 少抛射；C fallback 更少 → 大 [C/Fe]
**修饰**：
- "whose decay into Fe powers the SN light curve" 是 ⁵⁶Ni 的定语从句
- "because of the outer location of C" 给出 C 与 Fe 回落差异的物理根源

**中文**：暗淡 SN 起因于大量放射性 ⁵⁶Ni 的回落——其衰变成 Fe 驱动 SN 光变曲线。由于 C 位于更外层、回落少于 Fe，因此产生大 [C/Fe]。

---

### C4. HN 与 SN 产额对比

**原文**："If we compare the observations with the hypernova yield (black solid; M = 20 M☉, E₅₁ = 10) in Figure 4 (right), [(Ti, Co, Zn)/Fe] are larger and thus in much better agreement with observations than the SN yield."

**主干**：HN yield 与观测 [(Ti,Co,Zn)/Fe] 更好一致 vs. SN yield
**修饰**：
- "in Figure 4 (right)" 定位
- "thus in much better agreement" 中的 "thus" 由 "larger" 引出的因果

**中文**：若把观测与 Fig. 4 (right) 中的超新星产额 (M = 20 M☉, E₅₁ = 10) 对比，[(Ti, Co, Zn)/Fe] 更大，从而比正常 SN 产额与观测吻合更好。

---

### C5. 空洞 SN 的质量-能量关系

**原文**："For the faint SNe, the explosion energy was so small that most ⁵⁶Ni fell back onto the compact remnant. Thus the faint SN branch may become even a 'failed' SN branch at larger M."

**主干**：E 太小 → ⁵⁶Ni 大量回落 → 更大 M 处可能变成 failed SN
**修饰**：
- "so small that..." 表结果的 so…that 结构
- "onto the compact remnant" 给出回落的目标

**中文**：对于暗淡 SN，爆炸能量如此之小，以至于大部分 ⁵⁶Ni 都回落到了致密残骸上。因此在更大质量处，暗淡 SN 分支可能退化为"失败 SN"分支。
