## C. 长难句摘录（5 句）

### C1. §1 — Suess-Urey 的历史贡献

> "A basic and necessary step in the development of theories of stellar nucleosynthesis was made by Suess and Urey (1956), who selected astronomical, meteoritic, and terrestrial element abundance data and produced a table of cosmic abundances."

- **主干**: A step was made by Suess and Urey
- **修饰**:
  - "A basic and necessary step" 前置双形容词叠加,强调历史地位
  - "in the development of theories of stellar nucleosynthesis" 介词短语限定 step 的领域
  - "who selected ... and produced ..." 关系代词引导的定语从句,说明 Suess and Urey 的具体做法
- **翻译**: 在恒星核合成理论的发展中,Suess 和 Urey (1956) 迈出了基础性且必要的一步——他们选取了天文、陨石和地球的元素丰度数据,并产出了一张宇宙丰度表。

### C2. §3 — Fe 丰度争议

> "Nevertheless, it appears that the iron abundance determined from weak lines is higher than for the strong lines (L. Goldberg, private communication), and the iron abundance is anomalously high in the solar corona. Hence it has seemed best to use the meteoritic value."

- **主干**: it appears that ... is higher than ... and ... is ... high
- **修饰**:
  - "Nevertheless" 转折,承接前文"太阳强线 Fe 丰度低"
  - "(L. Goldberg, private communication)" 插入语引用未发表来源
  - "anomalously high" 双形容词,"anomalously" 强调异常性
  - "Hence" 因果连接,由上述两条证据推出方法论结论
  - "it has seemed best to" 显式表达主观判断(非确定性结论)
- **翻译**: 尽管如此,据 L. Goldberg 的私下交流,由弱线测定的铁丰度高于强线测定的值;而且铁丰度在日冕中异常偏高。因此,采用陨石值似乎是最优选择。

### C3. §9 — r-process 丰度平滑机制

> "Abundance smoothing processes must have been operative, either through a contribution from several values of atomic number Z to each mass number, or through frequent neutron emission following the high-energy beta decays of the neutron-rich final products of the capture process."

- **主干**: processes must have been operative, either through A or through B
- **修饰**:
  - "must have been operative" 情态动词短语,表达强推论(非直接观测)
  - "either through ... or through ..." 平行结构列举两种可能机制
  - "a contribution from several values of atomic number Z to each mass number" 机制 A: 多 Z 汇聚到同一 A
  - "frequent neutron emission following the high-energy beta decays of the neutron-rich final products" 机制 B: 富中子终产物的 β-衰变引发中子发射
- **翻译**: 丰度平滑过程必定在起作用,要么通过多个原子序数 Z 对同一质量数的贡献,要么通过俘获过程的丰中子终产物的高能 β-衰变后的频繁中子发射。

### C4. §9 — 两种 r-process 的区分

> "This suggests that there must be a very different fast time scale process in which a burst of neutrons can be produced and added to pre-existing intermediate elements. One possibility for producing this situation is the passage of a supernova shock wave through the helium shell in the presupernova structure, where rapid (α,n) reactions may be an effective neutron source."

- **主干**: This suggests that there must be a process ...; One possibility is the passage of ...
- **修饰**:
  - "a very different fast time scale process" 核心名词短语,修饰 "very different" 强调与主 r-process 的区分
  - "in which a burst of neutrons can be produced and added to pre-existing intermediate elements" 关系从句描述过程特征
  - "One possibility for producing this situation" 显式声明这是多种可能性之一(非唯一结论)
  - "where rapid (α,η) reactions may be an effective neutron source" 关系从句进一步限定环境
- **翻译**: 这暗示必然存在一种截然不同的快时间尺度过程,在其中能产生中子爆发并加到已存在的中等质量元素上。实现这一情景的一种可能是:超新星激波穿越前超新星结构中的氦壳层,其中快速的 (α,n) 反应可作为有效的中子源。

### C5. §9 — p-process 机制判据

> "Hence one should expect that the abundance distribution of the proton capture products would be similar to the superposition of the abundance distributions produced by neutron capture, except that the increasing Coulomb barrier with increasing atomic number would produce a progressive decrease in proton capture rates, with a corresponding increase in the ratio of the abundances of the neutron capture to proton capture products."

- **主干**: one should expect that A would be similar to B, except that C would produce D, with E
- **修饰**:
  - "one should expect" 客观化表述,弱化主观判断
  - "except that" 引入例外条件(库仑位垒)
  - "with a corresponding increase" 伴随状语,描述比例同步变化
  - 全句逻辑: 主断言(similar to 叠加) + 修饰因子(Coulomb barrier) + 观测可验证的推论(ratio 变化)
- **翻译**: 因此,人们应该预期质子俘获产物的丰度分布将与中子俘获产生的丰度分布的叠加相似,但库仑位垒随原子序数增大而上升会导致质子俘获率的持续下降,相应地,中子俘获产物与质子俘获产物的丰度比将同步上升。
