## C. 长难句摘录（3–5 句）

### C1. §6 结语（迁移悖论）
> "**The effects of element migration at the bottom of the convection zone** (which predict that present-day abundances, given by photospheric values, should be smaller by **10 to 15 percent** than the values in the solar nebula at the time of formation of the solar system, as given by meteorites) **are not observed**; this is puzzling although not surprising, **because** photospheric abundance uncertainties are still much too large to allow such a faint effect to be detected."

**主干**：effects of element migration are not observed; this is puzzling
**修饰**：which predict … 非限定定语从句详细说明预测；because … 原因状语从句给出作者解释
**翻译**：对流区底部的元素迁移效应（它预测现今光球丰度应比陨石给出的原太阳星云丰度低 10–15%）并未被观测到；这令人困惑但并不意外，因为光球丰度不确定度仍然太大，无法探测到如此微弱的效应。

### C2. §4.5（Fe 争议解决）
> "Using the **Holweger & Müller (1974)** empirical photospheric model and **65 Fe I lines**, one sees a **clear negative correlation** between derived A(Fe) and excitation potential; **by** lowering the temperature of the model by **~200 K at log τ ≈ −3**, the excitation-potential dependence disappears, and all lines converge on **A(Fe) = 7.50 ± 0.05**, **in agreement** with meteorites."

**主干**：one sees a clear negative correlation; the dependence disappears; all lines converge on A(Fe) = 7.50 ± 0.05
**修饰**：Using … 方式状语；by lowering … 方式状语说明修正；in agreement with meteorites 结果状语
**翻译**：使用 Holweger & Müller (1974) 经验光球模型和 65 条 Fe I 线，可看到导出的 A(Fe) 与激发势之间存在清晰的负相关；通过在 log τ ≈ −3 处将模型温度降低约 200 K，激发势依赖消失，所有线收敛于 A(Fe) = 7.50 ± 0.05，与陨石值一致。

### C3. §4.2（Li-Be-B 悖论）
> "The Sun is **depleted in Li by a factor 160** relative to the protosolar value (A_Li,prim ≈ 3.3 vs. A_Li,phot = 1.10), **whereas** Be and B are essentially undestroyed; **standard convection models cannot reproduce** this pattern, but **a mild mixing below the base of the convection zone** (Blöcker 1998; Vauclair 1998; Zahn 1998) successfully explains it."

**主干**：The Sun is depleted in Li by a factor 160; convection models cannot reproduce; mild mixing explains it
**修饰**：whereas 对比状语；parenthetical citation 补充作者；破折号前的长插入
**翻译**：相对原太阳值（A_Li,prim ≈ 3.3 对 A_Li,phot = 1.10），太阳的 Li 被消耗了 160 倍，而 Be 和 B 几乎未受破坏；标准对流模型无法复现这一模式，但对流区底部以下的微弱混合（Blöcker、Vauclair、Zahn）能成功解释它。

### C4. §3（兴趣点综述）
> "Solar chemical composition is **the benchmark** for **internal solar models** (through opacities of Fe at the center, and of O and Ne at the base of the convection zone), for **nuclear-synthesis theory** (which must reproduce the solar abundance pattern), and for **galaxy chemical evolution**."

**主干**：Solar composition is the benchmark for A, B, and C
**修饰**：三个 for 平行结构并列；每个 after 均有括号说明该领域为何依赖太阳丰度
**翻译**：太阳化学组成是太阳内部模型（通过 Fe 在中心的、以及 O 和 Ne 在对流区底部的不透明度）、核合成理论（必须复现太阳丰度模式）以及星系化学演化的基准。

### C5. §5（SAD 构建）
> "The **Standard Abundance Distribution** is constructed by combining **CI meteorite data** (accurate to 5–10% by modern laboratory techniques) with **solar values** for volatile elements (He, C, N, O, Ne, Ar); although isotopic anomalies are known to exist in the solar system, the SAD is remarkably similar everywhere and serves as the **cosmic abundances** reference."

**主干**：SAD is constructed by combining CI data with solar values
**修饰**：by combining … 方式状语；although … 让步状语；and serves as … 并列谓语
**翻译**：标准丰度分布通过将 CI 陨石数据（现代实验室技术可测到 5–10% 精度）与挥发元素（He、C、N、O、Ne、Ar）的太阳值相结合而构建；尽管已知太阳系内存在同位素异常，但 SAD 在各处都惊人地相似，并作为"宇宙丰度"的参考。
