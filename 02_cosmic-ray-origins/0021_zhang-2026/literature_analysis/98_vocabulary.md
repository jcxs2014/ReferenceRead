# 98. Vocabulary

## A. 学术逻辑词（Logical signposts）

| 原文词/短语 | 例句（摘自本文） | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| however | "However, a discrepancy has been observed between the measured spectra of Auger and the TA" | 转折：引出观测分歧 |
| in contrast | "In contrast, low-luminosity GRBs could dominate diffuse neutrino flux detected by IceCube" | 对比：HL vs LL GRB 反差 |
| while | "While high-luminosity GRBs are constrained… low-luminosity GRBs remain promising" | 让步：承认一边受限，强调另一边成立 |
| particularly | "AGN, particularly radio galaxies, remain promising sources" | 聚焦：从大类收窄到子类 |
| additionally | "Additionally, the rigidity distribution… provides further constraints" | 递进：补充第三条约束途径 |
| challenging | "challenging 'rare' steady sources" | 评价：指出约束对某一类源构成困难 |
| an open question | "An open question is whether the sources of UHECRs share similar properties" | 存疑：标记未决问题 |
| could / may | "Compact binary mergers may also contribute" | 弱断言：可能性而非定论 |

## C. 长难句（Complex sentences）

> **原句 1**（§3.1）:"Assuming the maximum magnetic luminosity is smaller than the bolometric luminosity, L_bol, the required minimum bolometric luminosity is L_bol,min ≳ 10⁴⁵ (Z/26)² (Γ/10)⁻² (β/0.2)⁻² erg s⁻¹, where Z is the nuclear charge, Γ is the Lorentz factor, and β is the dimensionless velocity of the source."
> **解析**：条件状语从句（假设磁光度<热光度）+ 主句（热光度下限公式）+ where 定语从句（三参数定义）。核心：把 Hillas 条件翻译为对源热光度的下限。

> **原句 2**（§3.2）:"Intermediate and heavy nuclei could be further enhanced during the acceleration process, with the enhancement scaling as (Z/A)², especially for particles with a large charge-to-mass ratio [49]."
> **解析**：主句（重核在加速中被增强）+ with 独立结构（增强随荷质比平方缩放）+ especially 状语（大 Z/A 粒子尤甚）。核心：解释为何观测成分偏重核——加速机制本身偏爱高 Z/A。

> **原句 3**（§4.4）:"The discrete shear acceleration process, a type of Fermi acceleration mechanism, results in CRs being accelerated and exhibiting a power-law energy spectrum."
> **解析**：主语（离散剪切加速过程）+ 同位语（Fermi 加速一类）+ 谓语（导致 CR 加速并呈幂律谱）。核心：剪切加速的机制归类与谱形预言。
