## 核心词汇（基于原文 Bell 1978 II 重 OCR 全文修订）

### A. 物理量与参数（原文定义）

| 符号 | 含义 | 原文定义位置 |
|---|---|---|
| $T_i$ | 注入粒子动能（初始能量） | §2, p.444: "initial energy must be for effective acceleration" |
| $u_1$ | 上游流体速度 | §1: "upstream fluid speed" |
| $u_2$ | 下游流体速度 | §1: "downstream fluid speed" |
| $v_s$ | 激波速度（$v_s = u_1$ for parallel shock） | §1: "shock front velocity" |
| $\delta = 4u_1/v$ | 每次穿越激波粒子逃逸概率 | §3: "proportion of particles lost" |
| $r_L = mv/qB$ | 拉摩半径（回旋半径） | §2: "radius of gyration" |
| $M$ | 马赫数 | §1: "shocks of high Mach number" |
| $v_A$ | Alfvén 速度 | §2: implicit in MHD treatment |
| $E_{\min}$ | 射电源最小能量 | §5: "minimum energy in radio sources" |
| $B_{\rm equip}$ | 能量均分磁场 | §5: "equipartition magnetic field" |

### B. 关键机制术语

| 术语 | 原文解释 | 页码 |
|---|---|---|
| **injection**（注入） | 粒子从热等离子体进入加速机制的过程；需 $T_i > T_{\rm th,post-shock}$ | §2 |
| **parallel shock**（平行激波） | 磁场平行于激波法向；粒子容易穿越 | §2 |
| **perpendicular shock**（垂直激波） | 磁场垂直于激波法向；粒子更难穿越 | §2 |
| **precursor**（前兆区） | 激波上游CR产生的Alfén波区域 | §2 |
| **diffusive shock acceleration**（扩散激波加速） | 粒子在激波两侧来回穿越，每次获得能量 $\Delta E/E \sim \delta$ | §3 |
| **injection energy**（注入能量） | 粒子须高于热能才能有效加速；本文 $T_i \sim 10$ keV | §2 |
| **equipartition**（能量均分） | 磁场能量密度 = 相对论粒子能量密度 | §5 |
| **gyroradius**（拉摩半径） | 粒子绕磁场回旋半径；$r_L = mv_\perp/qB$ | §2 |

### E. 长难句（原文节选）

> "In order to be accelerated, particles need to be able to pass through the shock without being strongly deflected and it is argued, using the Earth's bow shock as an example, that a shock front produces large numbers of protons, and probably electrons also, which satisfy this condition." （L11-15, Summary）

> "The energy necessary for injection depends on the structure of the shock front and especially on the shock thickness." （L37, §1）

> "The results of these sections are then combined in Section 4 to give an estimate of the number of high-energy particles produced in a supernova remnant and in Section 5 the radio emission is calculated." （L57-59, §1）

### F. 逻辑连接词（原文）

| 词汇 | 逻辑功能 | 原文例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **prior condition** | 强调必要条件 | "a prior condition being that the particles are sufficiently energetic" |
| **consequently** | 因果推导 | "consequently the acceleration mechanism is important" |
| **whereas** | 对比 | "relatively clear for protons, whereas for electrons less clear" |
| **in order to** | 目的 | "particles need to be able to pass through the shock in order to be accelerated" |
| **moreover** | 递进 | "moreover the acceleration mechanism applies equally to electrons" |
| **provided that** | 条件 | "provided that the particles satisfy the injection condition" |
