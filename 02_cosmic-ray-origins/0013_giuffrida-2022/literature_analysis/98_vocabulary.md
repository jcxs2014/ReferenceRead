# 98. Vocabulary — Giuffrida 2022

## A. Logical connectors

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| Several convincing indications have been obtained | 'Several convincing indications have been obtained in a few cases' | 引出证据 |
| confirming that | 'confirming that SNR shocks can accelerate hadrons' | 结果确认 |
| The identification of ... is still based on | 'The identification of SNRs as the main Galactic factory of CRs is still based on' | 引出主流观点 |

## B. 领域术语

- **SNR (Supernova Remnant)**：超新星遗迹，CR 的主要银河系加速源
- **SN 1006**：公元 1004 年观测的超新星（历史记载），Ia 型
- **DSA (Diffusive Shock Acceleration)**：扩散激波加速
- **π⁰ → 2γ**：中性π介子衰变 → GeV/TeV γ射线（强子加速特征）
- **X-ray synchrotron**：电子在激波处同步辐射（轻子加速特征）
- **Hillas criterion**：见 Hillas 1984
