# 98. Vocabulary — 学术词汇表

## A. Logical connectors + 原文例句

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| however | 'However, cosmic ray physicists have found it more difficult...' | 转折，引出核心困难（BE1987 Intro） |
| consequently | 'has become increasingly attractive as a consequence of both observational and theoretical developments' | 因果，连接观测与理论进展（BE1987 Intro） |
| in the case of | 'in the case of a supernova remnant...' | 条件举例，具体化一般论述（BE1987 Intro） |
| quite | 'quite high efficiency' | 程度修饰，强调效率之高（BE1987 Intro） |
| whereas | 'quite unlike the situation in the Galaxy, whereas in extragalactic...' | 对比，指出差异（BE1987 Intro） |
| naturally | 'Supernovae are naturally associated with particle acceleration' | 逻辑必然性，论证强度标记（BE1987 Intro） |
| in situ | 'in situ acceleration of relativistic particles' | 强调就地加速，非传输（BO1978 Abstract） |
| in this view | 'In this view, high energy particles are a natural by-product...' | 引出理论框架（BE1987 Intro） |

## C. 长难句分析

**BE1987 §Intro 第2段**（复合因果链）：
> 'Although the low energy particles...are mostly produced within the solar system, the higher energy particles must be produced beyond the solar system.'

- 句型：Although 让步从句 + 主句（演绎 necessity）
- 逻辑：排除法，排除太阳系内来源，确立银河系/河外起源

**BO1978 Abstract**（机制定义句）：
> 'High-energy particles in the vicinity of a shock are scattered by Alfvén waves carried by the converging fluid flow leading to a first-order acceleration process...'

- 句型：V-ing 短语作结果状语（leading to...）
- 逻辑：物理图像：散射→汇聚流→一阶加速
