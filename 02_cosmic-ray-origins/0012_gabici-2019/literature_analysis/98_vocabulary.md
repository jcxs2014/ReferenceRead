## C. 长难句摘录

### C1. §1 问题陈述

> "While it is firmly established that the bulk of CRs originates within the Galaxy, the way in which these particles are accelerated at their sources, as well as the way in which they are confined in the magnetized and turbulent interstellar medium (ISM) are still a matter of debate."

**主干**：the way ... are still a matter of debate
**修饰**：让步从句 "While it is firmly established..." + 两个并列主语（加速方式 + 约束方式）
**翻译**：虽然 CR 主体起源于银河系内已确立，但粒子如何在源处被加速、以及在磁化湍流 ISM 中如何被约束，仍是有争议的问题。

### C2. §5 结论

> "Thanks to impressive progress on the experimental side over the past ~15-20 years, both in direct and indirect observations, an enormous amount of data of unprecedented quality has allowed studying GCRs in greater detail. Overall, a much more complicated picture than previously thought has emerged."

**主干**：data ... has allowed studying GCRs; a more complicated picture has emerged
**修饰**：致谢状语 "Thanks to impressive progress..." + "both in direct and indirect"
**翻译**：得益于过去 15-20 年实验方面在直接和间接观测上的显著进展，前所未有的高质量海量数据使得对 GCR 的研究更加深入。总体而言，一个比以往所认为的复杂得多的图景已经浮现。
