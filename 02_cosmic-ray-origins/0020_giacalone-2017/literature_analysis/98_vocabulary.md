## C. 长难句

### C1. 极区聚集的核心机制
**原文**："The particles 'collect' at the poles as they approximately adhere to magnetic field lines that move poleward from their initial encounter with the shock at the equator, as the shock expands."

**主干**：particles collect at the poles
**修饰**：
- as they approximately adhere to magnetic field lines（方式）
- [field lines] that move poleward（定语从句修饰 field lines）
- from their initial encounter with the shock at the equator（起点）
- as the shock expands（时间条件）

**翻译**：粒子在极区"聚集"——它们大致贴附于磁场线，而这些场线随着激波膨胀，从粒子最初在赤道遭遇激波时开始向极区移动。

### C2. 注入阈值的对比
**原文**："In the limit θ=90°, Equation (1) reduces to $E_{\text{inj}} = E_R/[2(1+\kappa_\parallel/(A^2\kappa_\perp))]$, whereas in the limit θ=0°, we obtain $E_{\text{inj}} = 9\,E_R$."

**主干**：Eq. (1) reduces to A ... whereas ... we obtain B
**修饰**：两个极限（θ=90° 与 θ=0°）的对比
**翻译**：在 θ=90° 极限下，式 (1) 简化为 $E_{\text{inj}} = E_R/[2(1+\kappa_\parallel/(A^2\kappa_\perp))]$；而在 θ=0° 极限下，我们得到 $E_{\text{inj}} = 9\,E_R$。

### C3. 加速率沿激波面的变化
**原文**："The rate is fastest where the local shock-normal angle is 90° (locally perpendicular) and slowest where it is 0° (locally parallel), as discussed by Jokipii (1982, 1987), and also shown below in Figure 6(c)."

**主干**：rate is fastest where X and slowest where Y
**修饰**：两个 where 从句 + 文献引用 + 图表指引
**翻译**：加速率在局部激波法向角为 90°（局部准垂直）处最快，在 0°（局部准平行）处最慢，正如 Jokipii（1982, 1987）所讨论、并在图 6(c) 所示。

### C4. Lc/Rsh 参数的物理含义
**原文**："When Lc ∼ 1, the injection process is not local, and particles are injected with nearly the same efficiency at the quasi-perpendicular shock as they are at the quasi-parallel shock."

**主干**：injection is not local, and particles are injected with same efficiency
**修饰**：When 条件 + as 类比
**翻译**：当 $L_c/R_{\text{sh}} \sim 1$ 时，注入过程是非局地的，粒子在准垂直激波处的注入效率与在准平行激波处几乎相同。

### C5. 场线连接时间与极区聚集的因果链
**原文**："The field lines at the poles have been connected to the shock the longest. We also find that the highest-energy protons are initially accelerated near the equator or near the quasi-perpendicular portion of the shock, where the acceleration is more rapid."

**主干**：field lines have been connected the longest；protons are accelerated near equator
**修饰**：the longest（最高级）；where 从句（位置修饰）；more rapid（比较级）
**翻译**：极区的磁场线与激波连接的时间最长。我们还发现，最高能质子最初是在赤道附近或准垂直激波部分被加速的，那里的加速最快。
