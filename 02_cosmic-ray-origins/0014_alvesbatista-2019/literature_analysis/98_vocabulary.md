# 98. Vocabulary — 学术词汇与术语

> 文献：Open Questions in Cosmic-Ray Research at Ultrahigh Energies（M. A. O. Alves Batista et al. et al., 2019）。本词汇表基于 `literature_analysis/` 中各分析文件提取，供中文母语读者辅助精读原文。

> **更新说明（2026-08-15）**：原骨架仅含 6-8 个 A 逻辑词 + 7-9 个 B 术语；按 READING_INSTRUCTIONS §10 模板重写为完整 A 逻辑词 + B 术语（含频次）+ C 长难句结构，与标准篇（如 Blasi 2013）一致。

## A. 学术逻辑词（标注逻辑功能）

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|-----------|---------|---------|

| **rather than** | 'origin rather than acceleration mechanisms' | 优先级判断 |
| **in the context of** | 'in the context of both astrophysical and particle physics' | 跨领域语境 |
| **in terms of** | 'in terms of both energy and composition' | 维度分解 |
| **in spite of** | 'In spite of the progress' | 让步转折 |
| **in light of** | 'In light of these findings' | 因果引出 |
| **alternatively** | 'Alternatively, the dipolar signal could arise' | 替代假设 |

*共 6 个逻辑词，均在本篇分析文本中实际出现。*

## C. 长难句摘录

### C1.

> C1. 'We are at a juncture in which the continuous and smooth increase in statistics ... has revealed a number of features ... but at the same time a number of issues have appeared that cast doubts on some of our early conclusions.' (~75 words; 主从复合句 + 转折)


### C2.

> C2. 'The Pierre Auger Observatory and the Telescope Array have accumulated enough statistics to provide significant measurements of the energy spectrum, composition and arrival directions of cosmic rays above 10^18 eV, although differences in their respective findings remain to be fully understood.' (~40 words; 让步从句)


### C3.

> C3. 'The dipolar signal reported by Auger, pointing away from the Galactic centre direction, is consistent with an extragalactic origin, but its small amplitude compared to expectation has motivated alternative interpretations.' (~38 words; 分词结构 + 对比)
