## C. 长难句摘录

### C1. §1 UHECR 起源

> "Their origins are thought to be related to the most energetic phenomena in the Universe, such as relativistic jets and outflows associated with black holes, gamma-ray bursts and relativistic flares of active galactic nuclei, or large-scale accretion shocks around clusters of galaxies."

**主干**：origins are thought to be related to phenomena
**修饰**：四个并列候选（jets / GRB / AGN flares / accretion shocks）
**翻译**：它们的起源被认为与宇宙中最剧烈现象相关——与黑洞相关的相对论喷流和外流、伽马射线暴、活动星系核的相对论耀发，以及星系团周围的大尺度吸积激波。

### C2. §Summary 核心结论

> "We detected a particle with an energy of 244 (+29 stat.)(−76 syst.) EeV on 27 May 2021. The arrival direction of this event does not align with any known astronomical objects thought to be a potential source of UHECRs, even after taking into account deflection by the GMF under various assumptions."

**主干**：We detected a particle; The direction does not align with any known object
**修饰**：即使考虑 GMF 偏转（under various assumptions）
**翻译**：我们在 2021 年 5 月 27 日探测到一个能量为 244 EeV（统计误差 ±29，系统误差 +51/−76）的粒子。该事件的到达方向不与任何已知 UHECR 候选源天体对应，即使在各种假设下考虑了银河系磁场的偏转之后。
