# 98. Vocabulary — Hillas 1984

## A. Logical connectors

| 词汇/短语 | 原文例句 | 逻辑功能 |
|---|---|---|
| gradually | 'in gradual modes of acceleration' | 过程修饰 |
| clearly | 'Clearly, in gradual modes...' | 引出推论 |
| rather than | 'radiative losses rather than confinement' | 否定优先假设 |

## B. 领域术语

- **Hillas criterion**：$BL \gtrsim 10^{15}$ V，加速区域磁场×尺寸的约束
- **PeVatron**：能加速到 PeV（10^15 eV）的天体
- **UHECR**：Ultra-high-energy cosmic ray，>10^18 eV
- **Gyroradius $r_g = E/(ZeB)$**：粒子在磁场中的回旋半径
- **AGN**：Active Galactic Nucleus，活动星系核
- **GRB**：Gamma-ray burst，伽马射线暴
