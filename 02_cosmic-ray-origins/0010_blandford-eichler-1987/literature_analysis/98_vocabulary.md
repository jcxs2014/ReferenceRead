## C. 长难句摘录

### C1. §2.5（能量学约束句）

> "If, as is generally believed, the supernova rate in the galaxy is $\dot{S}_{\rm SN} \sim 3 \times 10^{-14}$ Myr$^{-1}$, then each supernova explosion must channel $\sim 3 \times 10^{49}$ erg or $\sim 3$ per cent of its total energy release into cosmic ray acceleration."

**主干**：then + each SN + must channel + $\sim 3 \times 10^{49}$ erg + into + CR acceleration
**修饰**：If 假设条件（"as is generally believed" 插入语），"of its total energy release" 修饰 3% 的参照量
**翻译**：如果，正如普遍认为的那样，银河系超新星率为 $\dot{S}_{\rm SN} \sim 3 \times 10^{-14}$ Myr$^{-1}$，则每次超新星爆发必须将 $\sim 3 \times 10^{49}$ erg，即其总能量的 $\sim 3\%$，注入宇宙线加速。
**逻辑功能**：条件句 + 因果推导——从超新星率和总功率反推单次注入能量需求。

### C2. §6.1（非线性 DSA 核心论点句）

> "We argue that cosmic rays form an essential component of the structure of astrophysical shocks and explain how such a small fraction of the incident particles can absorb such a disproportionate fraction of the energy."

**主干**：We argue + that + CR + form + essential component + of + shock structure；We explain + how + such small fraction + can absorb + disproportionate fraction
**修饰**：that 从句（宾语从句），how 从句（宾语从句），"such...such" 的对照结构
**翻译**：我们论证宇宙线构成天体物理激波结构的本质组成部分，并解释为什么如此少量的入射粒子能够吸收如此不成比例的份额能量。
**逻辑功能**：本文核心论点句——DSA 不仅是加速机制，CR 还是激波结构的组成部分。

### C3. §1（DSA 物理图像句）

> "They are accelerated systematically by scattering off the converging flow on either side of the shock front and, in the case of a supernova remnant, they can absorb the kinetic energy of the surrounding blast wave with quite high efficiency."

**主干**：They + are accelerated + by + scattering；they + can absorb + kinetic energy + with + efficiency
**修饰**：by 方式状语（scattering off the converging flow），介词短语作状语（in the case of...），"quite high efficiency" 程度修饰
**翻译**：它们通过散射激波面两侧的汇聚流体而系统地获得加速，而在超新星遗迹的情形下，它们可以高效吸收周围爆发现有的动能。
**逻辑功能**：DS 机制的一阶加速物理图像 + SNR 中的效率说明，两重因果。
