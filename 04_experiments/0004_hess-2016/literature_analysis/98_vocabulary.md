## C. 长难句分析

**例句 1**："Galactic cosmic rays reach energies of at least a few petaelectronvolts (of the order of 10$^{15}$ electronvolts); this implies that our Galaxy contains petaelectronvolt accelerators ('PeVatrons'), which must be located within the central molecular zone, given the diffuse γ-ray emission profile observed by HESS."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 主句 | Galactic cosmic rays reach energies | 主语 + 谓语 |
| of 介词 | of at least a few PeV | 介词短语给出能量量级 |
| 括号注释 | (of the order of 10$^{15}$ eV) | 数量级换算 |
| 分号 | ; | 分号前是观测事实，后是推论 |
| 主句推论 | this implies that our Galaxy contains PeVatrons | 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语从句 |
| which 引导 | which must be located... | 非限定性定语从句，给出位置约束 |
| given 引导 | given the diffuse γ-ray emission profile | 介词短语，给出观测依据 |

**例句 2**："The HESS collaboration observed a very-high-energy γ-ray signal from the inner ~200 pc of the Galaxy, with a spatial morphology and spectrum consistent with the decay of π⁰ mesons produced in inelastic collisions of cosmic-ray protons with molecular gas, indicating that the cosmic-ray energy density in this region must be at least ten times the local value."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 主句 | The HESS collaboration observed a VHE γ-ray signal | 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 |
| from 引导 | from the inner ~200 pc of the Galaxy | 介词短语给出空间范围 |
| with 引导 | with a spatial morphology and spectrum | 介词短语给出观测特征 |
| consistent with | consistent with the decay of π⁰ mesons | 表语，给出物理机制对照 |
| produced in | produced in inelastic collisions... | 过去分词短语修饰 π⁰ |
| of cosmic-ray protons | 介词短语修饰 collisions | 物理来源 |
| with molecular gas | 介词短语修饰 collisions | 介质 |
| indicating 分词 | indicating that the cosmic-ray energy density... | 现在分词，给出推论 |

**例句 3**："Such a high cosmic-ray energy density can be explained either by a supernova remnant that injects cosmic-ray protons into the molecular gas at a rate sufficient to compensate for the energy losses through inelastic p-p interactions, or, alternatively, by a distributed acceleration mechanism operating throughout the central molecular zone, with the latter possibility favored by the observed radial profile of the diffuse γ-ray emission."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 主句 | Such a high CR energy density can be explained | 主语 + 谓语 |
| either by ... or, alternatively, by ... | either ... or ... | 两种加速机制选择 |
| that 引导 | a SNR that injects CR protons | 定语从句修饰 SNR |
| sufficient to | sufficient to compensate for energy losses | 形容词短语给出充要条件 |
| operating 分词 | a distributed acceleration mechanism operating throughout the CMZ | 现在分词修饰 mechanism |
| with 引导 | with the latter possibility favored | 介词短语给出推论 |
