# §98 Vocabulary — 术语表

## A. 学术逻辑词（≥15 条）

| 单词 | 词性 | 逻辑功能 | 中文 | 原文例句 | 逻辑说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| however | adv. | 转折 | 然而 | "We report [...] however, the veto [...]" | 承认初步显著性，再承认系统性局限 |
| thereby | adv. | 因果 | 从而 | "thereby rejecting the atmospheric muon background" | 切伦科夫 veto 系统 → 排除大气 μ 背景 |
| in particular | adv. | 举例 | 特别是 | "In particular, the 28 events [...]" | 从总体观测聚焦到 28 个候选事件 |
| whereas | conj. | 对比 | 而 | "whereas the expected atmospheric background [...]" | 观测事件 vs 大气背景期望对照 |
| owing to | prep. | 因果 | 由于 | "owing to the absorption length of photons in ice" | 光子在冰中的吸收长度决定探测器深度 |
| accordingly | adv. | 因果 | 相应地 | "accordingly, the reconstruction [...]" | 选择条件 → 事例重建方法对应 |
| subsequently | adv. | 顺序 | 随后 | "Subsequently, the events were [...]" | 时序步骤：先选择后重建 |
| taken together | adv. | 综合 | 综合起来 | "Taken together, these events [...]" | 28 个事件汇总给出天体物理结论 |
| in addition | adv. | 递进 | 此外 | "In addition, the track topology [...]" | 补充级联形态信息 |
| despite | prep. | 让步 | 尽管 | "Despite the limited statistics [...]" | 承认统计有限，仍给出天体物理信号 |
| most importantly | adv. | 递进 | 最重要 | "Most importantly, two of the events [...]" | 强调 PeV 事件是关键证据 |
| namely | adv. | 列举 | 即 | "namely, charged-current νμ interactions" | 列举事例类型 |
| consequently | adv. | 因果 | 因此 | "Consequently, the astrophysical origin [...]" | 推导天体物理起源 |
| nevertheless | adv. | 转折 | 然而 | "Nevertheless, the absolute flux [...]" | 承认通量不确定性，仍给出上下限 |
| where | conj. | 条件 | 在…处 | "where the optical modules [...]" | 限定 IceCube 数字光学模块（DOM）的位置 |
| consistent with | adj. | 一致 | 与…一致 | "consistent with an astrophysical origin" | 比对天体物理起源假说 |

## B. 领域术语

| 术语 | 中文 | 释义 | 首现章节 |
|---|---|---|---|
| DOM（Digital Optical Module） | 数字光学模块 | IceCube 的基本探测单元——10″ 光电倍增管 + 采样电子学，埋于 1450–2450 m 深的南极冰中 | §1 |
| KM3NeT | 立方千米望远镜 | 海底中微子望远镜（地中海），IceCube 的姊妹项目 | §5 |
| Through-going muon | 穿透 μ 子 | 从下方穿过探测器的大气 μ，作为 veto 候选 | §2 |
| veto region | veto 区 | 探测器外层/顶部，标记为"非信号"的区域，用于排除大气背景 | §2 |
| νμ CC | νμ 带电流 | νμ → μ + X 的带电流相互作用，产生长 track | §3 |
| νe CC | νe 带电流 | νe → e + X 的带电流相互作用，产生级联（cascade） | §3 |
| ντ CC | ντ 带电流 | ντ → τ + X 的带电流相互作用，产生"double bang"事件 | §3 |
| NC | 中性流 | 中微子通过 Z⁰ 与核子散射，无轻子产生 | §3 |
| astrophysical flux | 天体物理流强 | 来自天体源的中微子流强（区别于大气/反应堆/加速器） | §5 |
| atmospheric background | 大气背景 | 由宇宙线轰击大气产生的 ν（conventional + prompt） | §4 |
| conventional atmospheric ν | 传统大气 ν | π/K 衰变产生的 ν（低能占主导） | §4 |
| prompt atmospheric ν | 瞬发大气 ν | 粲夸克衰变产生的 ν（高能主导） | §4 |
| zenith angle | 天顶角 | 中微子入射方向相对天顶的角度（cos θ：1=从天顶、-1=从地底） | §2 |
| all-sky | 全天 | 4π 立体角的全天球观测 | §5 |
| unfolding | 反卷积 | 用观测谱反推真实能谱的统计方法（本文 Bernlöhr et al. 参考） | §5 |

## C. 长难句分析

**例句 1**："The detection of these events is consistent with the discovery of a population of extraterrestrial neutrinos, with the observed flavor composition and isotropic arrival directions suggesting contributions from a combination of sources such as active galactic nuclei, starburst galaxies, and supernova remnants."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 主句 | The detection is consistent with the discovery | 主语 + 系动词 + 表语 |
| of 引导 | of these events | 介词短语修饰 detection |
| with 引导 | with the observed flavor composition... | 介词短语给出观测特征 |
| and 并列 | and isotropic arrival directions | 并列主语 |
| suggesting 分词 | suggesting contributions from... | 现在分词短语作伴随状语，给出物理来源猜测 |

**例句 2**："Although the statistical significance of the observed excess is limited by the small number of events, the energy spectrum, flavor composition, and arrival directions of the 28 events are incompatible with the expectations for atmospheric backgrounds at more than the 4σ level."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| Although 从句 | Although the statistical significance... | 让步状语从句 |
| 主句 | the energy spectrum, flavor composition, and arrival directions are incompatible | 主句三主语并列 + 系表 |
| with 引导 | with the expectations for atmospheric backgrounds | 介词短语，给出对比对象 |
| at 引导 | at more than the 4σ level | 程度状语，给出统计显著性 |

**例句 3**："The observed events with energies above 100 TeV, in particular the two events with energies of approximately 1 PeV, are most likely of astrophysical origin, as their deposited energies, angular distributions, and flavor compositions cannot be readily explained by any known atmospheric background component."

| 句子成分 | 内容 | 语法功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 主句 | The observed events are most likely of astrophysical origin | 主语 + 系表 |
| with 引导 | with energies above 100 TeV | 介词短语修饰主语 |
| in particular 插入语 | in particular the two events... | 强调性插入语 |
| as 引导 | as their deposited energies... | 原因状语从句 |
| 否定并列 | cannot be readily explained | 双否定加强"超出大气背景" |
